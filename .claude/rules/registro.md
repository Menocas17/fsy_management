**Registro (escáner de llegadas y capacitaciones):** `CheckinsController` + `checkin_scanner_controller.js`, offline-first (roster and queue in localStorage per mode). Access: `User#checkin_registrar?` (full access, director_logistica, logística in the check-in area). Only one registro (the arrival or one training) is active at a time: `ScanWindow` keeps it in `AppSetting`, chosen with mutually exclusive switches in Configuración by `User#scan_manager?` (superadmin, director_logistica); the scanner page has no selector and opens the active one. Scans taken offline while the previous registro was active still sync after switching (`previous_scan`). A registration made in the line can be voided ("Anular" under the camera for the last scan, or in the recent list) with a reason from `CheckinsController::VOID_REASONS` plus optional detail; the void travels in the same queue (`kind: "void"`) so it works offline and in order, only deletes the record created by that scan (`target_token`) or the one picked (`record_id`) — never an earlier real arrival — and writes `AuditLog` category `registro`. Anyone may open a ficha by scanning a badge (`ScansController`, dashboard "Escanear gafete"); registering stays with the registrars. On phones the camera opens full screen (`.scan-viewer[data-fullscreen]` in `application.css`, a fixed layer rather than the Fullscreen API, which iPhone only grants to `<video>`): counter, the result card (with its own «Anular»), «Código» for typing a badge in a dialog, ✕ to leave and «Pantalla completa» to come back; leaving it is remembered for the visit. Android back/Esc close it through `CloseWatcher`, never a pushed history entry (popping it made Turbo reload the scanner); a screen wake lock holds while it's open. «Finalizar registro» (arrival page only, `User#scan_manager?`, `CheckinsController#finish`/`#reopen`, `PATCH`/`DELETE /registro/finalizar`) sets `CompanyReveal.open_to_jovenes!`: jóvenes stop seeing «Próximamente» and see their company, and one `Alert` to role joven goes out once; «Reabrir» hides it again without an alert. Both write `AuditLog` category `registro`; the scanner stays open for late arrivals.
